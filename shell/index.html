Playing on a touchscreen

  • Drag anywhere to run. Nano heads in the direction you push, and turns when your finger swings around.
  • Jump — tap again in mid-air for a double jump.
  • Fire — hold for continuous fire. Tap the weapon icon to switch.
  • Tap near an egg or rock to pick it up. Tap again to throw.
  • Second finger drag left or right to swivel the camera, up or down to zoom.
